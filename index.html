About

Engineering advanced composite systems from process to performance.

I am a doctoral student in Mechanical Engineering at Utah State University. My research centers on additive manufacturing of polymer composites for automotive and aerospace applications, with an emphasis on mechanical and microstructural characterization and process–structure–property analysis.

My work spans sheet-lamination and material-extrusion additive manufacturing, resin infusion and vacuum bagging, and the characterization of composite microstructure, mechanical behavior, thermal response, and surfaces. Across sponsored projects, I have worked on lightweight vehicle structures, urban air mobility propellers, and protection materials for electrified pavement systems.
